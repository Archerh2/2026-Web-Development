Computer Programming and Web Design

The Computer Programming and Web Design (CPWD) program at Bayside High School is designed to equip students with the skills and knowledge necessary to pursue careers in the rapidly evolving field of technology. The program offers a comprehensive curriculum that covers various aspects of computer programming, web development, and design principles. Students will learn programming languages, software development techniques, and web design tools, preparing them for future opportunities in the tech industry. The CPWD program emphasizes hands-on learning, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills, ensuring that students are well-prepared for both higher education and the workforce.
